What's new

What changed in AppliedIn, newest first.

v1.2.0 Job search and one-click apply

Job search

Multiple job boards, one place

Find jobs offers Fresh, Career Ops search and the Cosign network. Each names its sources, and every result shows the board its posting lives on.

Cosign network

About 70,000 open roles

Search by title, cities, salary and remote. All of the US by default, filled to about a hundred matches from your preferences.

One-click apply

Select all, apply in one press

Each role is scored first, and only roles that clear your match bar are tailored and submitted. You're asked only when a form needs an answer.

Live steps

Follow every application

Score, Tailor, Check, Apply and Submitted, with a readable timeline. Open any application in the pipeline in its own window.

  • Career Ops search redesigned: one search composer, results beside the role you're reading, and your applications in the same column.
  • A refreshed dashboard: consistent motion, a status bar in the header, calmer Needs you cards and icon links.
